ISCEB • Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your ISCEB Account & Data

Institute of Supply Chain Excellence Bangladesh (ISCEB) • Package: com.aistudio.supplychain.scm

Students and users of the ISCEB Android app and Web portal can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time using either self-service deletion inside the app or via direct support request below.

Open ISCEB App Portal (Profile → Delete Account) Privacy Policy

Option 1: Instant Self-Service Deletion (Inside Android App or Web Portal)

  1. Open the ISCEB Android app (or ISCEB Web Portal) while signed in to your student account.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom or side navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the Privacy Policy & Account Data card.
  4. Tap Delete Account (or Delete My Account & Data on Web) and confirm permanent deletion.
  5. Your account session signs out immediately and your personal records are erased from this device and Firebase Cloud Firestore.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed on your device, send a deletion request from your registered email address:

  • Email To: admin@isceb.com or isceb25@gmail.com
  • Include: Your registered full name, email address, and Student ID (if known).
  • Processing Time: Requests are verified and completed within 7 business days.
Send Deletion Email Request

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Immediately: Your student profile (name, email, mobile number, organization, designation, enrolled program, photo), Firebase Authentication user link, per-class learning progress, personal certificates subcollection, and assignment submissions.
  • Academic Integrity & Audit Retention: Non-revocable credential verification records in the global registry (when an official PGD Diploma has already been issued) or immutable exam audit timestamps may be retained solely for employer certificate verification unless revoked by an ISCEB Administrator.
