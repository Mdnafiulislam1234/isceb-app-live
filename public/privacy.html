Privacy Policy

Institute of Supply Chain Excellence Bangladesh (ISCEB) — App & Web Portal

Effective Date: November 27, 2026  |  App Package: com.aistudio.supplychain.scm

This Privacy Policy explains how ISCEB (Institute of Supply Chain Excellence Bangladesh) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the ISCEB Android application and official web portal.

Open ISCEB Portal Account & Data Deletion Guide App Distribution Page

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Details: Full name, email address, optional mobile number, company/organization, designation, enrolled PGD program, and generated Student ID.
  • Google Sign-In Authentication: When you choose Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication / Credential Manager), we receive your Google UID, display name, email address, and profile avatar URL to synchronize your learning workspace across devices.
  • Optional Profile Photo & Study Files: Photos or assignment attachments you explicitly pick using the Android system Photo Picker or File Picker. We do not scan or access unrelated storage folders.
  • Learning & Assessment Records: Lecture completion status, active study duration, PDF reading confirmation, quiz/exam attempts and scores, assignment submissions, and issued diploma certificates.

2. How We Use Information

  • To enroll students and display accurate names and Student IDs on PGD Supply Chain Management certificates.
  • To track 48-lecture completion, module exams, assignments, and certification eligibility.
  • To synchronize curriculum updates, notices, and student progress securely across Android and Web via Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • To allow authorized ISCEB faculty and administrators to review submitted assignments and verify diploma authenticity.

No Advertising or Data Selling: ISCEB does not display third-party ads, does not use ad tracking SDKs, and never sells or rents personal data to third parties.

3. Storage, Security & Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and protected by zero-trust Firestore Security Rules restricting access to account owners and authorized ISCEB faculty/administrators.
  • On-Device Cache: Preferences and offline progress cache remain stored privately inside the application sandbox so you can study smoothly.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your account. You may permanently delete your student account and personal data directly inside the ISCEB app (Profile → Privacy Policy & Account Data → Delete Account) or by following our Account Deletion Guide.

Upon self-service deletion, your personal profile, contact info, saved photo, course progress, personal certificate subcollection, and assignment submissions are deleted immediately from both your device and Firebase Cloud Firestore.
